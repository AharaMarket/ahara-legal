1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to people who create an Ahara account, join a restaurant team on Ahara, or otherwise interact with the Service. We collect only the information needed to operate the Service for you and your restaurant team. Categories of data:

3. How we use information

  • To operate the Service — sign you in, sync your restaurant’s data across teammates’ devices, and deliver the features you interact with.
  • To send operational notifications (push and, where you have opted in, SMS) about events that matter to your restaurant: stock alerts, shopping-list approvals, order updates, team chat.
  • To extract structured line items from invoice photos you upload, using AI processing.
  • To diagnose crashes, debug issues, and improve reliability and performance.
  • To detect abuse and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for cross-context behavioral advertising and we do not show third-party ads in the Service.

5. Data retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Operational data (catalog, inventory, invoices, lists, orders, chat) is retained while the restaurant’s team continues to use Ahara. When you delete your account (see Section 7) we delete or de-identify the personal information tied to that account within 30 days, subject to limited retention for legitimate business reasons (e.g. tax records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution).

6. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including transport-layer encryption (HTTPS / TLS) for data in transit, encryption at rest on our cloud-storage providers, role-based access controls on our backend, and Firebase Security Rules enforced at the database and storage layer. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your profile and your restaurant’s data from inside the app at any time.
  • Account deletion — you can delete your account directly from the app: Account → Delete account. The action signs you out, removes your profile and authentication record, and starts the deletion of personal data associated with your account. You may also email support@ahara.app to request deletion.
  • Push notifications — you can disable push notifications at any time in your device’s system settings. The Service remains fully functional without push notifications.
  • SMS notifications — you can opt out by texting STOP to the sending number, or by removing your phone number from your account profile. Text HELP to the sending number for assistance.
  • Photos, camera, contacts — the app only accesses these when you initiate an action that requires them (uploading an invoice photo, scanning a receipt, inviting a teammate by phone). You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • Regional rights — depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (US/CA). These can include the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to lodge a complaint with a regulator. To exercise any of these rights, email support@ahara.app. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. International data transfers

We operate primarily in the United States, and your information may be processed and stored on servers located in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect personal data transferred from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact support@ahara.app and we will delete the information.
